## 🏗️ Framework Architecture

### 10 Core Modules

The framework is built on 10 interconnected modules that cover the entire clinical data lifecycle:

1. **Study Design & Planning**: Sample size, power analysis, randomization scripts.
2. **Site & Subject Management**: Enrollment tracking and forecasting.
3. **Randomization & Blinding**: Automated list generation and validation.
4. **Data Processing (SDTM/ADaM)**: Direct conversion of raw data to CDISC standards.
5. **Statistical Analysis**: Pre-built models for ANOVA, Survival, MMRM.
6. **TLF Generation**: Automated Tables, Listings, and Figures.
7. **Validation & Quality Control**: CDISC compliance and statistical QC.
8. **Monitoring & Tracking**: Interactive Shiny dashboards for real-time progress.
9. **Documentation**: SAP and CSR template automation.
10. **Regulatory Submission**: eCTD structure and Define.xml generation.
